Purpose-Built for University Campus Communities.

LostLink organizes lost and found recovery through structured campus reporting, minimizing friction between students and campus front desks.

  • Guided Multi-Step Reporting: High-resolution photo upload, exact timestamp recording, and hall selection.
  • Visual Attribute Correlation: Filters by brand, category, physical markings, and color tags.
  • Challenge Verification: Protects property by requiring accurate verification before contact information is shared.
